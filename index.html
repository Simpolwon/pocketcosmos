About the studio

Pocket Cosmos makes short animated videos that explain space and science in plain words, with a small astronaut called Pip. Pocket Cosmos Studio is the desktop app the channel owner uses on his own computer to produce those videos and publish them to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

The studio is a private tool. It is used only by the channel owner, on the channel's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

How the studio uses TikTok

The studio uses two TikTok products: Login Kit, to connect the channel's TikTok account, and the Content Posting API, to publish the channel's short videos to that account.

  1. The owner presses Connect TikTok in the studio, signs in on TikTok's own page and approves the permissions user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.
  2. When a Short is ready, the studio opens a Post to TikTok screen. It shows the connected account's name and picture, a preview of the video and its caption.
  3. The owner chooses who can see the video from the options TikTok allows for the account. Nothing is chosen in advance.
  4. The owner decides whether to allow comments, Duet and Stitch. All three start switched off, and any the account has turned off in TikTok are greyed out.
  5. If a video promotes a brand, the owner switches on the disclosure and picks Your brand or Branded content, and the matching TikTok label is shown.
  6. The owner reads the Music Usage Confirmation notice and presses Post. Only then is the video uploaded. The studio checks the post's status and shows when it is live.

Videos are made with the help of AI tools, so every post is marked as AI-generated content. The studio never posts without the owner pressing Post, never posts to anyone else's account, and never reads other users' data.

Privacy Policy

The studio handles only the channel owner's own account data. It does not collect, see or store information about viewers or any other TikTok users.

What is stored
The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when the owner connects the account, the account's ID, and the display name, username and profile picture used to show which account is connected.
Where
Only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent anywhere except to the platform the data came from.
Why
To upload the owner's videos to the owner's account when he presses Post, and to check whether an upload has finished.
How long
Until the account is disconnected. Pressing Disconnect in the studio deletes the stored tokens and profile details.
Your control
Press Disconnect in the studio, or remove the app's access in your TikTok account settings.
