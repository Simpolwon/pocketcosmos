Big science in small, animated bites.

Pocket Cosmos is an animated science channel. Pip, a small astronaut with a star on his helmet, explains space, the body and the strange physics of everyday life in short videos you can watch in a few minutes.

Watch on YouTubeFollow on TikTok

What we make

Every episode starts with one question and answers it in plain words, with hand-crafted animation, a narrator and Pip along for the ride.

Episodes

Long-form explainers

Ten-to-fifteen-minute stories on YouTube, told chapter by chapter, from the microbes inside you to the edge of the universe.

Shorts

One idea, one minute

Vertical clips on YouTube Shorts and TikTok that pull the single most surprising fact out of each episode.

Pip

A guide, not a lecturer

Pip notices, wonders, gets things wrong and tries again, and moves things with a little telekinesis when the science needs a hand.

Topics on the channel

The body

The trillions of microbes that live in and on you, what they do, and why most of them are on your side.

Space

Galaxies, black holes, planets and the scale of everything, made small enough to picture.

Everyday physics

The hidden science in ordinary things: kitchens, bathrooms, weather and the stuff in your pocket.

Behind the channel: Pocket Cosmos Studio

The videos are produced in Pocket Cosmos Studio, the owner's own desktop app. It turns a script into an animated episode and, when the owner approves, publishes it to the channel's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.
